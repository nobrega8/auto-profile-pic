Auto Profile Pic

To show pictures, this add-on needs to request them from external services. Nothing is sent until you allow it.

What is sent, and to whom:

  • A SHA-256 hash of each sender's email address to Gravatar (gravatar.com), to find their profile picture.
  • The domain name of senders without a Gravatar (e.g. company.com) to Google (google.com/s2/favicons) and, as a fallback, DuckDuckGo (icons.duckduckgo.com), to download their company logo. Personal email providers are skipped.

Nothing else is sent: no message content, no names, no full email addresses, no usage data. Results are cached locally in Thunderbird.

Without permission, only address book photos and initials are shown. You can change this at any time in the add-on's options.
