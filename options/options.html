Privacy

Picture sources

Tried in order. The address book photo always comes first; without any picture, the sender's initial is shown.

Personal email domains

No company logo for these: the provider's logo says nothing about the sender. One per line.

Cache

Pictures are kept for 30 days (7 days when none is found). Gravatar receives a hash of the email address; Google, or DuckDuckGo as a fallback, receive the domain name.
